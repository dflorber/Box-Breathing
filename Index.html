Tactical SEALFIT Box Protocol

What is Box Breathing?

Popularized by retired Navy SEAL Commander Mark Divine, Box Breathing (also known as four-square breathing) is a tactical physiological tool deployed before high-stress operations to downregulate the autonomic nervous system.

1. INHALE (4s) Slowly through the nose into the lower belly. Expand the diaphragm.
2. HOLD FULL (4s) Maintain lungs full without clenching throat. Stay relaxed.
3. EXHALE (4s) Smooth, controlled release through nostrils or lips.
4. HOLD EMPTY (4s) Hold gently with empty lungs. Rest in the quiet stillness.

Key Physiological Benefits:

  • Parasympathetic Tone: Stimulates the vagus nerve, immediately slowing heart rate.
  • Cortisol Reduction: Flushes stress hormones and mitigates fight-or-flight paralysis.
  • CO₂ Tolerance: The pauses increase cellular tolerance to carbon dioxide, optimizing oxygen absorption.
Posture Tip: Sit tall with spine straight, shoulders dropped away from ears, chin parallel to deck, and palms resting comfortably on thighs.
